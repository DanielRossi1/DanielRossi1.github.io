:seedling: **New Publication!** Our paper "[BoltNet: An Ultra-Lightweight Convolutional Network for On-Device Plant Species Identification](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11844)" has been accepted at the **CVPPA** workshop (Computer Vision in Plant Phenotyping and Agriculture) at **ECCV 2026**.

BoltNet introduces a _Spatial Redistribution Bottleneck_ and _Logit PreSampling_ to tackle very large taxonomic spaces on constrained hardware. On **Pl@ntNet-300K** it reaches a **0.682 F1-score with only 341K parameters (1.37 MB)**, the best F1 among the evaluated models below 2 MB, and it was benchmarked end-to-end on CPU, GPU and NPU across Raspberry Pi 5, Jetson Orin Nano and Hailo-8.

**Authors**: Daniel Rossi, Guido Borghi, Roberto Vezzani
**Preprint**: [arXiv:2608.11844](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11844)
