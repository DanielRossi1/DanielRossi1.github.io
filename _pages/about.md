I am a final-year PhD Candidate in Artificial Intelligence at the [University of Modena and Reggio Emilia](https://www.ingmo.unimore.it/it), working at [AImageLab](https://aimagelab.ing.unimore.it/) under the supervision of Prof. Roberto Vezzani.

My research focuses on **Efficient Deep Learning** and **Edge AI deployment**. I design ultra-lightweight neural networks for computer vision, and I study how they actually behave once they leave the workstation: latency, energy per inference and accuracy on NPUs, embedded GPUs and microcontrollers. Application domains include **aerial disaster response**, **surveillance**, **on-device fine-grained recognition** and **monocular depth estimation**.

Recent work includes [TakuNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05880) (WACV 2025 Workshops), an energy-efficient CNN reaching more than 650 fps on a 15 W Jetson Orin Nano, its journal extension in [Image and Vision Computing](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.imavis.2026.106151), and [BoltNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11844) (CVPPA @ ECCV 2026), a 341K-parameter network for on-device plant species identification benchmarked across CPU, GPU and NPU. I also maintain open-source tooling for this line of work, such as [AutoDock](https://github.com/DanielRossi1/AutoDock) and [EdgePowerMeter](https://github.com/DanielRossi1/EdgePowerMeter).

Previously, I received my Master's Degree _cum laude_ with a thesis entitled "3D Human Pose Estimation from RGB Images with Transformers and Deformable Attention". During this period, I also had the opportunity to work for 6 months as an intern at [Toyota Motor Europe](https://www.toyota-europe.com/) in Brussels, focusing on NeRF, Gaussian Splatting, 3D Pose and Hand Estimation.

Outside the lab I run [Projecto](https://www.youtube.com/@ProjectoOfficial), a YouTube channel about electronics, embedded systems and deep learning.
